# Villagers

The people of Wehlsbüll, what they say, and what they think of the player.

- [villagers.md](villagers.md): Who the villagers are, the village, sheets, behaviour, what is saved.
- [routines.md](routines.md): Homes, workplaces, own interiors, activities and the planned day, pathfinding.
  Decided; the activities and the default day are built (#7, steps 1 to 9), the model's planner is not.
- [inside.md](inside.md): Villagers go into their house, the church and their workplace, and are seen in the
  interior (#8). Decided and built.
- [travel.md](travel.md): Villagers travelling where the world isn't loaded (#68). Planned, not
  decided.
- [dialogue.md](dialogue.md): Talking: the chat, the language model, the prompt, memory.
- [gossip.md](gossip.md): What villagers see, remember and pass on, and how they think of the player by it. Decided, not built.
- [reputation.md](reputation.md): Reputation, and how it spreads.
- [quests.md](quests.md): Small goals the villagers give, from files in data/quests/, and the notice board (#12).
  Decided and built.
- [relationships.md](relationships.md): Ten friendship levels, what moves them and what they open. Decided, being built (#48).
- [ideas.md](ideas.md): Ideas for NPCs, behaviour and LLM dialogue. Not decided.
