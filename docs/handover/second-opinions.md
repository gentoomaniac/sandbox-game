## 2026-11-11: task 185 plan (gradual season overlays), Sol review

Plan: scratchpad plan185.md (Plan agent, Opus 5.5). Sol verdict: **sound with changes**.

What Sol wants changed:
- Cover must reach paths, soil, floodplain and wetland too. Today snow draws the grass row over every terrain, so a grass-only shader can't replace it.
- Terrain atlas UVs: terrain sources keep Godot's texture padding, so disable it consistently or convert the coordinates. Anchor the cover in world space, including the dual-grid half-tile offset.
- Materials don't compose. Fold the cover into the existing object, decoration, water and fire shaders instead of overwriting them. SoilWetness has 24 child layers that each need it.
- Snow-only assets should be explicit masks with ranks, not "snow minus bare" differences. Bare trees have other silhouettes. Sample at the object's ground anchor, and match the sway and falling rotation.
- The ring texture needs a validity contract: negative modulo, the seam, stale slots, teleports, `ChunkResidency.require()` outside the window, and houses kept outside residency. It is a render cache only.
- Drop `melt_at` at first; SnowCover already melts, so it would count melt twice.
- The GroundCover mirror should share exact inputs, quantisation and rounding, and give a coverage fraction rather than a tile-centre boolean.
- Global uniforms: register them early, budget the samplers, keep data textures out of colour conversion. `make bench` is headless, so it can't show a GPU gain.
- Make the migration additive. Separate the daily vegetation refresh (foliage, flowers, fruit) from weather rendering first, then move the ground, decorations, objects and houses one at a time, and remove the old code last.
