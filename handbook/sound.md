# Sound and music

What you hear in the game, and how to turn it up or down.

## Volume

- Settings, Audio has Master, Effects, Ambience and Music volumes. Music at 0 turns it off.
- "Sound in background" off: the game is silent while its window is not focused.
- While the game is paused (pause menu, settings, a chat, a choice) the world's sounds fade out
  and return when you go on. Only the music and the menu's clicks play.

## Effects

- Footsteps follow the walk and the ground: grass, soil, sand, wetland, ice, snow, a wooden
  floor.
- Every tool, pickup, building and menu has a sound, played from where it happens.
- What you do is heard when it worked. An action the game refuses only says "denied".
- Inside a house the weather and the outside world are muffled and faint.
- A room with a hearth crackles, louder the nearer you stand (all through a small house, only
  near the fire in a big one).

## Music

- Two calm pieces of chiptune instruments: "Tide", a slow sway, and "Harbour evening", a
  shanty-like tune.
- Each loops three times, then the other crossfades in on the next beat.
- The first start takes a second or two before the music begins; later starts are quicker.
- There is no music in test runs (`--mute`) or headless.

## Ambience

- Rain and wind follow the weather and swell with gusts.
- Thunder follows each flash by how far the strike was: a far rumble many seconds later, now
  and then a close crack almost at once.
- Birds chirp by day; deer, rabbits and squirrels are heard; bees buzz at their flowers.
- Frogs croak near water on warm spring and summer evenings, in a quiet chorus at dusk.
- Storks clatter their bills, geese honk, crows caw.
- Farm animals moo, baa and cluck now and then; a rooster crows at dawn and now and then through
  the day.
